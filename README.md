# Famous Last Words at 1 Percent

Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Famous Last Words at 1 Percent"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-famous-last-words-at-1-percent-63e2b0b4): write the funniest possible one-line farewell a phone could say as its battery hits 1 percent.

## The entry

> I've been at 1 percent since lunch. I just love watching you sprint for the charger like the house is on fire. Worth it.

120 characters, one line. The twist: the phone was never really dying — it faked the 1 percent all afternoon just to watch its owner panic. The phone speaks in its own voice, about its own battery, and the punch ("Worth it.") lands at the very end.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
